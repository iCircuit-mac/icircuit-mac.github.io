iCircuit

The easiest way to design and experiment with circuits on macOS. Advanced simulation engine handles analog and digital circuits with realtime always-on analysis. Perfect for students, hobbyists, and engineers.

Always Simulating

Unlike traditional CAD, iCircuit simulates continuously—like working with a real powered circuit. Add elements, connect them, adjust properties, and see results instantly. No pausing for measurements or reports.

Comprehensive Components

Over 30 elements at your fingertips: resistors, switches, MOSFETs, digital gates, and more. Build anything from simple prototypes to complex designs effortlessly.

Precise Measurements

Integrated multimeter probes voltages and currents in real time. Built-in oscilloscope tracks multiple signals over time with automatic modes for instant insights.

Seamless Export

Export circuits as PNG, PDF, or SVG for reports and websites. Scope data exports for offline analysis.

How It Works

1. Build Your Circuit

Drag and drop components from the library. Connect wires intuitively, just like sketching on paper.

2. Simulate Live

Power is always on. Tweak values, flip switches, and watch behavior unfold in real time.

3. Measure and Analyze

Use the multimeter for instant reads. Add signals to the oscilloscope for time-based waveforms.

4. Export and Share

Save designs or export visuals and data for presentations, reports, or further work.
